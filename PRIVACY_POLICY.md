# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2 August 2026_

Thank you for using **Solve 'em** ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains what
information the App handles, how it is used, and the choices you have.

The App is a math learning companion. It helps you photograph a math problem
and get step-by-step solutions, check your own written work, chat with a tutor,
and work offline with a calculator, formula library and notes.

## 1. Summary

- The App has **no accounts** and **does not collect your name, email or phone**.
- Your **API key never leaves your device**.
- **Photos and math content are sent only to your chosen AI provider**, and only
  when you actively analyze or check a problem.
- Your **history and notes are stored locally on your device**.
- You can **delete all data at any time** from inside the App.

## 2. Information the App handles

### a) AI API key

To use the AI-powered modes you provide an API key for a provider of your choice
(OpenAI-compatible endpoints, Google Gemini, OpenRouter or NVIDIA NIM). The key
is stored **locally on your device** and is used only to authenticate requests
made by the App to that provider. The key is never logged, uploaded elsewhere or
shared with any party other than the provider you configured.

### b) Photos and math content

When you use AI Mode or Control Mode, the App may process a photo you take:

- **On-device OCR**: Text in the image is read using on-device Google ML Kit and
  does not leave your device.
- **AI analysis**: The image or extracted text is sent to the AI provider you
  configured so that provider can generate a solution or check your work. This
  transfer is subject to that provider's own privacy and data policies. Please
  review them before use.

### c) Local data

Solutions you save, your chat history, math notes and app settings are stored
locally on your device (using local storage). The App does not upload this data
to any server operated by the developer.

### d) Diagnostics and analytics

The App does not include analytics, advertising SDKs or crash-reporting services
that collect personal data.

## 3. How the App uses information

Information is used **only to provide the App's features**:

- to run on-device OCR and display extracted math text,
- to call the AI provider you chose to generate solutions, checks or chat
  replies,
- to save and restore your local history and notes.

## 4. Data retention and deletion

- Data stored locally stays on your device until you delete it.
- You can **clear your API key**, **clear your history** and **delete your notes**
  at any time from the App's Settings screen.
- Uninstalling the App removes locally stored data.

## 5. Third-party services

The App can connect to AI providers you configure (for example OpenAI, Google
Gemini, OpenRouter or NVIDIA NIM). When you use those providers, the content you
submit (photos, extracted text, messages) is processed by them under their own
terms and privacy policies. We encourage you to review:

- OpenAI: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
- Google (Gemini): https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/privacy
- OpenRouter: https://openrouter.ai/privacy
- NVIDIA (NIM): https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/privacy-center/

## 6. Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because
the App has no accounts and stores data locally, no personal information is
gathered by the developer.

## 7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be
reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
